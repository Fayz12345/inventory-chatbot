Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 2026

Who we are

Bridge Wireless ("Bridgewireless", "we", "us", or "our") is a reseller of refurbished and used mobile devices. We operate an internal inventory and pricing platform, hosted at ai.bridge-renew.net, that we use to manage our device inventory and to list products for sale on online marketplaces, including eBay, Amazon, Best Buy, and Reebelo. Our company website is bridge-wireless.com.

This policy explains what information we collect and process in connection with that platform and our marketplace sales, and how we handle it.

Information we collect and process

  • Internal operational data. We maintain our own inventory and pricing information for the devices we sell — for example, device models, identifiers, condition grades, quantities, and competitive pricing data. This is business data generated by our own operations.
  • Order and buyer information from marketplaces. When we sell a device through a marketplace such as eBay, we receive order and buyer information from that marketplace — for example, the buyer's name, shipping address, contact details, and order details. We receive this information solely to fulfill and support the order.

How we use information

We use the information described above to:

  • process, fulfill, and ship orders;
  • provide customer support and communicate about orders;
  • handle returns, replacements, and warranty claims;
  • meet our legal, tax, and accounting obligations.

We do not sell buyers' personal data, and we do not use it for purposes unrelated to fulfilling and supporting their orders.

Marketplace and eBay data

Our platform integrates with eBay's APIs to list products and manage the resulting orders. We handle eBay account and order data in accordance with eBay's policies and applicable law, and only for the purpose of fulfilling and supporting orders placed through eBay.

When we receive an eBay Marketplace Account Deletion / account closure notification, we delete the associated buyer data we hold for that account, except where we are required to retain certain records to meet legal, tax, or accounting obligations.

How we share information

We share order and buyer information only as needed to fulfill and support orders, namely with:

  • shipping and logistics carriers, to deliver orders;
  • the marketplaces and payment processors that handle the transaction;
  • authorities or other parties where we are required to do so by law.

We do not otherwise sell or rent personal information to third parties.

Data retention

We keep personal information only for as long as it is needed to fulfill orders, provide support, and satisfy our legal, tax, and accounting obligations. When it is no longer needed for these purposes, we delete or anonymize it.

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to request access to, correction of, or deletion of the personal information we hold about you. To make such a request, please contact us using the details below and we will respond in accordance with applicable law.

Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in our practices or for legal reasons. When we do, we will revise the "Last updated" date at the top of this page. We encourage you to review this page periodically.
